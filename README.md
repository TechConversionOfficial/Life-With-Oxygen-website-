## About Life With Oxygen

[Life With Oxygen](https://lifewithoxygen.com/) is a wellness-focused shopping website associated with **Diatomic Healthcare**. The store markets products featuring the **Ox66®** ingredient and groups its catalogue into skincare, nutraceuticals and an oxygen-related book. Its publicly visible products include **Esquore Oxygen Mist**, **Esquore Serum**, **O2Boost Dietary Supplement**, **O2Boost Oxygen for Sport Supplement** and **Nurturing the Nature**.

The site's message connects wellness products with a broader brand narrative about energy, beauty, lifestyle and athletic activity. Its visitors may be looking for a skincare product, researching the brand's supplement range, or seeking clarification before they consider an order. This makes clear navigation and an evidence-conscious explanation of the products especially important.

**Health-claim note:** The business website makes claims about oxygen-based formulations, safety, efficacy and certification. Those are **client-published product/marketing statements**; this portfolio README neither independently verifies them nor recommends supplements as treatment for a health condition.

## The Website Challenge

Health and wellness ecommerce has a different decision journey from a general retail store. A shopper needs product names, clear categories, prices, variants, appropriate usage information, ordering policies and a trustworthy way to contact the business. Beyond buying, some visitors arrive with questions about how a product works or whether its promotional claims have supporting documentation.

A website in this category must balance two tasks: **make the catalogue easy to shop** and **make health-related content easy to distinguish from medical advice**. The public project record shows how these needs are addressed in the published website. It does not establish that a particular older website was defective or that a redesign caused measured commercial improvement.

## How the Published Website Addresses the Brief

1. **Introduce the brand and its products immediately.** The homepage displays its oxygen-focused positioning beside representative skincare and supplement products, followed by a clear shopping action.
2. **Keep product discovery organised.** The public store separates **All Products, Skin Care, Oxygen Books and Nutraceuticals**, while the Shop page allows browsing the catalogue.
3. **Bring product shopping into the homepage.** Visitors can see named products and move to product options or add-to-cart actions rather than navigating through a long informational funnel first.
4. **Offer an additional educational path.** The site features an **Oxygen Deficiency Scoring Tool (ODST™)**, described by the business as a self-assessment wellness indicator. It is **not a medical diagnostic test**, and its health interpretations should not be treated as validated clinical measurements.
5. **Support understanding with explanatory sections.** The homepage includes a brand-science narrative, product-related explanations, testimonials and descriptions of claimed certification.
6. **Provide normal ecommerce account and transaction routes.** Search, account access, cart, order tracking and links to shipping and refund policies are present in the public navigation or footer.
7. **Allow visitors to reach the business.** Contact details and a contact page provide an alternative to completing a shopping action immediately.

These are **observations from publicly accessible website content**. They should not be read as a complete internal list of development tasks or an independently tested checkout audit.

## Quality, Accessibility and Responsible Health Content

The following are **suggested evaluation references**, not assertions that the specific website holds certifications or passed formal audits:

- [Google Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals): check real-user loading, responsiveness and layout stability for the homepage, catalogue and product pages.
- [WCAG 2.2 Level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/): assess navigation, contrast, readable product information, accessible controls and the scoring tool's keyboard usability.
- [Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials): review relevant product content, appropriate indexing and useful shopping information.
- [Schema.org](https://schema.org/): evaluate accurate product, offer, organisation and breadcrumb structured data where implemented.
- **Medical and advertising accuracy:** review health-related marketing claims, testimonials, product disclaimers, substantiation and applicable Indian food, drug and consumer advertising requirements before publishing or changing product content.
- **Consumer privacy:** assess any personal information collected through accounts, checkout and the wellness questionnaire, including applicable privacy notices and data minimisation.

**Clinical distinction:** The website's ODST™ questionnaire is explicitly introduced as a wellness indicator. The score should not be described as measuring blood oxygen saturation, cellular oxygen utilisation or a medical diagnosis without separate validation. Users seeking medical guidance should consult qualified healthcare professionals.
